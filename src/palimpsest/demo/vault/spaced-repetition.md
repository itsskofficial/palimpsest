# Spaced repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals produces far better long-term retention
than reviewing it the same number of times in one sitting. The effect is one of the
largest and most reliably replicated in the psychology of learning.

## Why it works

The usual explanation is retrieval difficulty: a review that is slightly hard
strengthens the memory more than one that is easy. Spacing makes each review harder
than the last would have been, without making it so hard that recall fails.

## In practice

- The first interval matters most, though I have never pinned down how soon it
  actually needs to be.
- Failing a card and re-learning it is not wasted effort; it is the mechanism.
- Interleaving different topics helps, though it feels worse while doing it.
